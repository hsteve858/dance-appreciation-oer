# Sway 5: Dance Genres as Cultural Reflections
*Unit 3, Week 3*

## Sources
- Lisa Smith, "Dance and Cultural Meaning: An Introduction to the Anthropology of Dance" *(main source)*
- "Cultural Fusion – The Intersection of Traditional and Contemporary Dance," De Mode Global
- Ivor Guest and Marion Kant, "Ballet," *Encyclopædia Britannica*
- Hanna Järvinen, "A Cultural History of Ballet"
- Huiqiao Yang, "The Fusion of Traditional and Modern Dance"
- Naowarat Buddee, "How Choreometrics Reflects Self-identity and Self-concept Through Cultural Dance"
- Fiveable, "1.3 Dance as a Form of Cultural Expression"

## Summary
Frames dance genres as cultural artifacts using Lisa Smith's anthropological framework (Franz Boas, ethnochoreologists Gertrude Kurath, Katherine Dunham, Pearl Primus). Walks through ballet as European court culture and hip-hop as urban resistance as two contrasting case studies, then assigns a presentation on a chosen genre's cultural origin and evolution.
