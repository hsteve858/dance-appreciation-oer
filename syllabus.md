# DAN 1025: Dance Appreciation — Syllabus

## Course Description
This course introduces students to the discovery, experience, analysis, and evaluation of different genres of dance. Students explore music, choreography, costuming, history, and culture in relation to dance.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1. Develop an understanding and appreciation of dance as a cultural and artistic form.
2. Discuss various dance genres and the history and cultural influences behind these dance forms.
3. Experience different dance styles and expand their knowledge of dance through active participation.
4. Critically review dance performances with an emphasis on movement, choreography, and cultural context.
5. Demonstrate competency in reading, writing, and speaking about dance with clarity within historical and cultural frameworks.

## Required Resources
- Primary Resource: "The RRCC Dance Appreciation Toolkit"
- Additional materials: library articles, videos, and digital resources provided on the course platform.

## Weekly Topics
| Week | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Introduction to Dance as a Cultural and Artistic Form |
| 2 | Understanding the Roles in Dance |
| 3 | The Value of Dance Genres in Society, History, and Culture |
| 4–5 | Dance Genres and Movement Styles |
| 6 | Language of Dance and Principles of Movement |
| 7 | Reflection and Critique of Dance Performances |
| 8 | Midterm Exam and Review |
| 9–11 | Deep Dive into Specific Dance Genres |
| 12 | Dance in Society and Cultural Perspectives |
| 13 | Choreography and the Creative Process |
| 14 | Final Project Presentations |
| 15 | Course Wrap-Up and Final Exam Preparation |

## Assessment and Grading
- Class Participation: 15%
- Dance Performance Critique Assignment: 20%
- Midterm Exam: 20%
- Final Project Presentation: 25%
- Final Exam: 20%
