# Sway 10: Postmodern and Experimental Dance
*Unit 4, Weeks 4–5*

## Sources
- Emily Macel They, "Mastering Trisha Brown's Modern Dance Style"
- Riikka Laakso, "Merce Cunningham – 65 Years of Rethinking Choreography and Artistic Coexistence"
- Michael J. Kramer, "The Mind Is a Muscle: Postmodern Dance and Intellectual History"
- Seattle Met, "Four of the Most Influential Postmodern Dances"
- Merce Cunningham Trust, "Merce Cunningham"
- Beth Weinstein, "The Collaborative Legacy of Merce Cunningham: Architecture and Dance"

## Summary
Covers postmodern dance's rejection of formal technique in favor of chance procedures, pedestrian movement, and site-specific performance, through key figures Merce Cunningham (chance, music/dance separation), Trisha Brown (pedestrian movement, non-stage venues), Yvonne Rainer (process over product), and Steve Paxton (Contact Improvisation).
