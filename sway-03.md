# Sway 3: The Choreographer & Dancer
*Unit 2, Week 2*

## Sources
- Myron Howard Nadel, "Choreography: Separating the Dancer from the Dance"
- PBS Thirteen, "Camille A. Brown & Dancers" (NYC-ARTS Profile)
- Good Morning America, feature on Twyla Tharp

## Summary
Introduces the choreographer's role through interviews with Camille A. Brown and Twyla Tharp, then Nadel's article on distinguishing the choreographer's vision from the dancer's execution. Journal prompts ask students to identify choreographer responsibilities and the choreographer–dancer relationship.
