## Summary
Opens with Oke's article on the near-impossibility of archiving dance's ephemeral, embodied nature, then formally defines the elements of Space (levels, directions, pathways), Time (tempo, rhythm, duration), and Energy (dynamics, movement quality), plus how music and dance relate across different genres.
