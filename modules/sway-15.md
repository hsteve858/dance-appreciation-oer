# Sway 15: Ballet
*Unit 7, Week 9*

**[View SWAY PRESENTATION](https://sway.cloud.microsoft/DOYjnC95pi3N52Ui?ref=Link){:target="_blank"}**

## Summary
A deep dive into ballet's history from Italian Renaissance court dance through Louis XIV's formalization, Noverre's dramatic ballet d'action, the Romantic era's ethereal ballerina, and Russia's imperial dominance. Profiles pioneering choreographers (Bournonville, Balanchine, Béjart, Ashton) and legendary dancers (Pavlova, Nureyev, Fonteyn, Baryshnikov), covers classical/romantic/neoclassical/contemporary ballet styles, major companies worldwide, and ballet's cross-cultural adaptations (India, China, Guinea's Les Ballets Africains).
