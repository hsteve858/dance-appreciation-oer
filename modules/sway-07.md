# Sway 7: Introduction to Dance Genres & Performance Dance
*Unit 4, Weeks 4–5*

**[View SWAY PRESENTATION](https://sway.cloud.microsoft/yOO9SgQUHbKof9fg?ref=Link){:target="_blank"}**

## Summary
Defines what qualifies a dance style as a "genre" — distinct movement vocabulary, historical context, cultural significance, musical relationship, aesthetic principles, training methods, and performance context. Students then present on ballet, modern/contemporary, or jazz/tap and compare two genres in writing.
