# Sway 18: Hip Hop
*Unit 7, Week 11*

**[View SWAY PRESENTATION](https://sway.cloud.microsoft/IEwlqMlAUPVcB59o?ref=Link){:target="_blank"}**

## Summary
Traces hip-hop dance's dual origins — B-boying/uprocking in 1970s New York (Kool DJ Herc, top rockin', footwork, power moves) and locking/popping on the West Coast (Don Campbell, Sam "Boogaloo Sam" Solomon) — plus its foundational four elements (DJing, MCing, graffiti, knowledge) and the role of battles and media exposure in its evolution and spread.
