# Sway 11: Language of Dance and Anatomy of Movement
*Unit 5, Week 6*

**[VIEW PRESENTATION](https://sway.cloud.microsoft/Vfe1uL4OozGYoAwU?ref=Link){:target="_blank"}**

## Summary
A vocabulary-building lecture covering the five ballet foot positions, counting/rhythm conventions, general studio terms (mark, spotting, canon, musicality), common steps (plié, relevé, tendu, battement), stage direction terms, and the "merde" good-luck tradition, paired with anatomy basics: major muscle groups, joint types, and alignment principles used in dance training.
