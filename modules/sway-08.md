# Sway 8: Social Dance and Introduction to Somatic Practices
*Unit 4, Weeks 4–5*

[View SWAY PRESENTATION](https://sway.cloud.microsoft/jgz73jfBa4GCQSNd?ref=Link){:target="_blank"}

## Summary
Splits into two halves: social dance (salsa, bachata, merengue, swing, ballroom, hip-hop/house) as participatory, community-building movement rather than performance; and somatic dance practices (Alexander Technique, Feldenkrais, Body-Mind Centering, Ideokinesis, Authentic Movement) as body-awareness and healing-oriented approaches. Closes with a comparison of performance, social, and somatic dance.
