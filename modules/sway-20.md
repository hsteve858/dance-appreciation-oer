## Summary
Returns to Nadel's article to examine the choreographer's role as creator, director, and visionary — distinguishing choreography (the structural "blueprint") from performance (the dancer's execution of it). Covers the creative process, the compositional elements of space/time/energy, and includes analysis of a Bill T. Jones/Arnie Zane Company excerpt before a group choreography project.
