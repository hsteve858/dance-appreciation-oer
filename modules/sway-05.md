# Sway 5: Dance Genres as Cultural Reflections
*Unit 3, Week 3*

## Summary
Frames dance genres as cultural artifacts using Lisa Smith's anthropological framework (Franz Boas, ethnochoreologists Gertrude Kurath, Katherine Dunham, Pearl Primus). Walks through ballet as European court culture and hip-hop as urban resistance as two contrasting case studies, then assigns a presentation on a chosen genre's cultural origin and evolution.
