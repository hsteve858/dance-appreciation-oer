*Unit 3, Week 3*

## Summary
Traces dance as both a unifying and dividing social force — from Wolz's "world dance" concept through the racialized suppression and reinvention of African American dance (tap, jazz), 1950s generational rifts (rock and roll vs. ballroom), and dance as activism (civil rights, stepping, Black Lives Matter, LGBTQ+ ballroom culture, and TikTok-era protest choreography).
