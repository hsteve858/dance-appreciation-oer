## Summary
Covers the audience as an active participant rather than a passive observer — shaped by personal history, emotional state, and cultural background. Contrasts silent concert-dance audiences with participatory forms (immersive theater, ballroom/vogue culture, cultural ceremonies), then has students write an audience-perspective critique of a self-chosen dance video.
