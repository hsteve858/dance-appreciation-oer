# Sway 17: Jazz and Tap
*Unit 7, Week 10*

## Sources
- Myron Howard Nadel, "Jazz and Tap"
- Ksenia Parkhatskaya, "Jazz. A Brief Cultural History and Characteristics of Black Dance"
- "The History of Tap Dance," CLI Studios
- "The Lindy Hop," The Kennedy Center
- "Ann Miller | The Best Tap Dancer You've Never Heard Of," Rubi Leandro
- Cathy Ritchie, "Eleanor Powell: Born to Dance"

## Summary
Covers the fusion of African and European traditions that produced American jazz and tap, including the fraught role of minstrel shows and vaudeville. Profiles key styles (buck and wing, soft-shoe, eccentric dance, Lindy Hop) and dancers/choreographers from Bill "Bojangles" Robinson and the Nicholas Brothers through Jack Cole, Katherine Dunham, Bob Fosse, and contemporary tap innovator Savion Glover.
