# Sway 8: Social Dance and Introduction to Somatic Practices
*Unit 4, Weeks 4–5*

## Sources
- R. Austin Marquez, "STAGES: Social Dance Forms as Catalyst for Recovery"
- Myron Howard Nadel, "Social Dance: A Portrait of People at Play"
- Rangel Spirov and Veronika Chernyavska, "What Are the Most Popular Social Dances?"
- Glenna Batson (IADMS), *Somatic Studies and Dance*
- Hannah Kay Andersen, *Dance Science and Somatics in Training and Performance*
- Ruth Levin, "Twelve Principles for Powerful Somatic Practice"

- Somatic Trauma Recovery Center, "The Power of Somatic Dance Movement in Trauma Therapy"

## Summary
Splits into two halves: social dance (salsa, bachata, merengue, swing, ballroom, hip-hop/house) as participatory, community-building movement rather than performance; and somatic dance practices (Alexander Technique, Feldenkrais, Body-Mind Centering, Ideokinesis, Authentic Movement) as body-awareness and healing-oriented approaches. Closes with a comparison of performance, social, and somatic dance.
