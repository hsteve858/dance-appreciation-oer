## Summary
Covers dance's role in community ritual and identity — birth ceremonies (Middle Eastern/North African, Igbo), funeral and burial traditions (West Africa, Madagascar's Famadihana), Native American powwows, and East African tribal dances (Maasai, San, Gome) — closing with krumping and clowning as expressions of urban Los Angeles youth culture born from Tommy the Clown's community-building response to gang life.
