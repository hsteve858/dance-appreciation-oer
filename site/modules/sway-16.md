# Sway 16: Modern & Contemporary Dance
*Unit 7, Week 9*

## Sources
- Myron Nadel and Marc Strauss, "Modern and Contemporary Dance"
- Linda Mae, "6 Choreographers Who Influenced Contemporary Dance"
- Barbra Whitney, "William Forsythe," *Encyclopaedia Britannica*
- "Trisha Brown," *Encyclopaedia Britannica*

## Summary
Traces modern dance from Isadora Duncan and Loïe Fuller's break from ballet, through the Denishawn company, to second-generation pioneers Martha Graham (contraction/release) and Doris Humphrey (fall/recovery), then postmodern figures Cunningham and Brown, ending with contemporary dance's fusion of styles and William Forsythe's deconstruction of classical technique.
