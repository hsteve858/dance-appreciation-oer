# Sway 9: World Dance Traditions
*Unit 4, Weeks 4–5*

## Sources
- Ferne Yangyeitie Caulker-Bronson, "African Dance: Divine Motion"
- "Top 10 Best Traditional Dances in Africa"
- Pali Chandra, "What is Kathak? History and Evolution of Gharanas of Kathak"
- Shylu Winston, "Sadhir Attam | History of Bharatanatyam"
- Lois Jaeck Marie, "The Body as Revolutionary Text: The Dance as Protest Literature in Latin America"
- Mariano Neris and Bella Malekian, "Rumba Cubana"
- Helen Myers, "Piercing the Mask of Japanese Dance Theater"

## Summary
Surveys global dance traditions — African dance's Ashé and mind-body-spirit unity, Indian classical forms (Bharatanatyam, Kathak) and their symbolic mudras/mythology, and Latin American dances (Cuban rumba, Dominican merengue) as vehicles for national and racial identity politics.
